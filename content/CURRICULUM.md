# English Flow — Curriculum Map (DRAFT for review)

18 modules × 8 episodes = **144 texts**. A1: 5 modules, A2: 6, B1: 7.
How to write each text: [CONTENT_GUIDE.md](CONTENT_GUIDE.md).

Grammar is cumulative: a module may use its own focus plus everything from
earlier modules. "Traps" refer to section 7 of the guide.

---

## A1 — first steps (40 texts)

### A1-1 · New Flatmates
- **Story:** Lena (24, from Kazan, junior designer) moves into a shared flat in
  Manchester with Tom (27, chef, messy) and Priya (22, student, very organised).
  Rooms, objects, house rules, and a mystery: who keeps eating Lena's yoghurt?
- **Topics:** home, introductions, countries, numbers, colours.
- **Grammar:** *to be*, *have got*, *there is / there are*, *a / an / the*, plurals, possessive 's.
- **Note:** the first module for absolute beginners: texts 40–80 words. Home
  words are limited to the most common ones (*room, kitchen, table, bed, door,
  window, fridge*), not *wardrobe, cupboard, chest of drawers*.
- **Traps:** 1 articles, 4 uncountables (*furniture*), 10 *people are*.

### A1-2 · The Café Shift
- **Story:** Max (19, from Berlin) gets his first job at a tiny café in
  Brighton run by Rosa (45, strict but kind). Orders go wrong, a regular
  customer is a mystery, and a food critic might be coming.
- **Topics:** food and drink, prices and money, time, polite requests.
- **Grammar:** present simple (I/you/he/she, questions and negatives with *do/does*), *can / can't*, *Would you like…?* as a chunk.
- **Review:** *to be*, *there is / are*, articles (A1-1).
- **Traps:** 3 questions with *do/does*, 1 articles.

### A1-3 · Lost in London
- **Story:** Two friends, Dasha (21, from Minsk) and Sofia (22, from Lisbon), have
  one weekend in London, one dead phone and a list of places to see.
- **Topics:** transport, directions, city places, tickets, weather.
- **Grammar:** prepositions of place and time, imperatives, question words, *How much / How many*.
- **Review:** present simple questions and negatives (A1-2), *there is / are* (A1-1).
- **Traps:** 8 prepositions, 3 questions.

### A1-4 · The Group Chat
- **Story:** Six friends plan a surprise birthday party for Jamal via a group
  chat (mixed with real-life scenes). Nobody agrees on anything. Jamal is
  starting to suspect.
- **Topics:** days, months, hobbies, likes and dislikes, clothes, family.
- **Grammar:** present continuous vs present simple, *like / love / hate + -ing*, object pronouns.
- **Review:** prepositions of time (A1-3), *can* (A1-2).
- **Traps:** 5 *agree*, 3 questions.

### A1-5 · 30-Day Challenge
- **Story:** Ollie (31, office worker) and his gran Margaret (72) both sign up for
  a 30-day fitness challenge. Margaret is winning.
- **Topics:** body, health, daily routine, sport, food habits.
- **Grammar:** adverbs of frequency, *was / were*, past simple of regular verbs plus 10 common irregulars (*went, had, did, got, saw, made, came, said, ate, felt*).
- **Review:** present continuous (A1-4), present simple (A1-2).
- **Traps:** 7 *win vs earn*, 1 articles.

## A2 — real life (48 texts)

### A2-1 · The Job Interview
- **Story:** Kirill (26, from St Petersburg, developer) has a week to prepare for
  his first interview at a London company. His flatmate coaches him; the
  interview goes very differently from the plan.
- **Topics:** jobs, skills, CV, describing experience, workplaces.
- **Grammar:** past simple (all common irregular verbs), *could / couldn't*, past time expressions (*ago, last…*).
- **Review:** frequency adverbs and *was / were* (A1-5), present continuous (A1-4).
- **Traps:** 7 *make vs do*, 2 (past simple side), 9 false friends (*actual*).

### A2-2 · Festival Weekend
- **Story:** Four friends at a music festival in the rain: a lost tent, a band
  that might be famous, and a phone with 4% battery.
- **Topics:** music, camping, weather, plans, problems.
- **Grammar:** future forms: *going to* (plans), present continuous (arrangements: *We're meeting at six*), *will* (decisions and predictions).
- **Traps:** 6 no future after *when / if*.

### A2-3 · Swipe Right
- **Story:** Anya (28) tries a dating app for the first time, with very honest
  commentary from her best friend Chloe. Three dates; one is a disaster, one is
  surprising.
- **Topics:** describing people, personality, feelings, food and restaurants.
- **Grammar:** comparatives and superlatives, *too / enough*, adjectives -ed / -ing (*bored / boring*).
- **Review:** future forms (A2-2), past simple (A2-1).
- **Traps:** 9 false friends (*sympathetic*), 3 questions.

### A2-4 · Flat Hunting
- **Story:** Jake and Mira (both 25) need a flat in Bristol in two weeks. A
  landlord who never answers, a flat that is too good to be true, and bills.
- **Topics:** renting, money, bills, problems in the home, neighbours.
- **Grammar:** countable / uncountable, *some / any / much / many / a lot of*, *should / have to / don't have to*.
- **Review:** comparatives (A2-3), future forms (A2-2).
- **Traps:** 4 uncountables (*advice, information*), 7 *borrow vs lend*.

### A2-5 · The Influencer Experiment
- **Story:** Nastya (20, student) bets her brother she can get 10,000 followers
  in a month. Fake trends, a viral mistake, and the question of what's real.
- **Topics:** social media, phones, online shopping, apps, the internet.
- **Grammar:** present perfect with *ever / never / just / already / yet*.
- **Review:** *should / have to* (A2-4), comparatives (A2-3).
- **Traps:** 2 present perfect vs past simple.

### A2-6 · Road Trip to Scotland
- **Story:** Three colleagues drive from London to the Highlands for a work
  event: a tiny car, a wrong turn, sheep on the road, and a secret one of them
  is keeping.
- **Topics:** travel, nature, food, accidents and problems, accommodation.
- **Grammar:** past continuous vs past simple (*I was driving when…*), first conditional, *might* for possibility.
- **Review:** present perfect (A2-5), uncountables (A2-4).
- **Traps:** 6 *when / if*, 8 prepositions (*arrive in / at*).

## B1 — independence (56 texts)

### B1-1 · The Startup
- **Story:** Three friends quit their jobs to build an app. Investors,
  deadlines, an argument that might end the friendship.
- **Topics:** work, meetings, emails, money, teamwork.
- **Grammar:** present perfect vs past simple, present perfect continuous.
- **Review:** first conditional (A2-6), present perfect basics (A2-5).
- **Traps:** 2, 7 *say vs tell*, 9 false friends (*decade*).

### B1-2 · Mystery at the Hostel
- **Story:** At a hostel in Edinburgh, a guest's passport disappears. Ruslan (29,
  traveller from Almaty) decides to find out who took it.
- **Topics:** travel, people and descriptions, crime vocabulary (light), routines.
- **Grammar:** modals of deduction (*must / might / can't be*), past perfect.
- **Review:** present perfect vs past simple (B1-1), past continuous (A2-6).
- **Traps:** 10 *police are*, 1 articles.

### B1-3 · Burnout
- **Story:** Emma (33, nurse) and Lev (35, accountant) are neighbours who both hit
  a wall at work. They try to change their lives in very different ways.
- **Topics:** mental health, sleep, stress, habits, work-life balance.
- **Grammar:** *used to*, gerunds and infinitives (*stop doing / try to do*).
- **Review:** past perfect (B1-2), present perfect continuous (B1-1).
- **Traps:** 5 *agree*, 4 *advice*.

### B1-4 · Podcast Hosts
- **Story:** Two students start a podcast about strange news stories. Their
  episode about a local legend gets them into trouble.
- **Topics:** media, news, fake news, interviews, opinions.
- **Grammar:** passive (present and past simple), reported speech (statements and questions).
- **Review:** *used to* (B1-3), modals of deduction (B1-2).
- **Traps:** 7 *say vs tell*, 10 *news is*, 9 *magazine*.

### B1-5 · The Green Street
- **Story:** Neighbours on one street in Leeds try to make it greener, against a
  developer who wants to build a car park.
- **Topics:** environment, city change, community, local politics (non-partisan).
- **Grammar:** second conditional, *wish + past simple*, zero conditional.
- **Review:** passive (B1-4), gerunds and infinitives (B1-3).
- **Traps:** 6 *if*, 8 *depend on*.

### B1-6 · Family Dinner
- **Story:** Masha (27) brings her British boyfriend to meet her family, who have
  moved from Moscow to London. Three generations, one table, many opinions.
- **Topics:** family, relationships, generations, cultural differences, food traditions.
- **Grammar:** relative clauses (*who / which / that / where*), common phrasal verbs (about 20, recycled through the module).
- **Review:** second conditional (B1-5), reported speech (B1-4).
- **Traps:** 1 articles, 9 false friends (*sympathetic*), 3 questions.

### B1-7 · One Year Abroad
- **Story:** A year in the life of Ilya (30), who moved to the UK: paperwork,
  culture shock, homesickness, small victories, and deciding whether to stay.
- **Topics:** bureaucracy, banks and doctors, culture, identity, the future.
- **Grammar:** *should have / could have* (regret), plus a planned review of every earlier grammar focus (each episode revisits 2–3).
- **Traps:** all 10, as a final review.

## Vocabulary budget

144 texts × about 6.5 key words ≈ **950 words and chunks taught through reading**.
B1 needs roughly 2,000–2,500 words in total, so reading covers about 40%.
The rest comes from the level flashcard sets. For that to work, **the level
word lists are written first** and texts take at least 70% of their key words
from them (guide §7). Flashcard words then meet the learner in context, and
reading words feed the flashcards.

The structure is built to grow: more texts can be added later as extra
modules or "side stories" per level without changing anything here.

## Coverage check

Topics spread across modules: home · food · work (×3) · travel (×4) ·
city · social life and relationships (×4) · health (×2) · media and internet (×2) ·
money (×3) · nature and environment (×2) · culture and family (×2) ·
bureaucracy · music · sport.

## Build order

1. Starter list + A1/A2/B1 word lists (with Russian translations).
2. Code changes from guide §14, plus the automatic checker.
3. Pilot: A1-1, reviewed by you; adjust the guide.
4. Remaining modules, in order. Order matters, because "known words" and
   recycling depend on earlier modules.
