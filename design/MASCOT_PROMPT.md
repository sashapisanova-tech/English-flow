Design a mascot family for **LingoFlow**, a series of language-learning apps:
**English Flow** (British English for Russian speakers) and **Dutch Flow** (Dutch),
with more languages later. Each app gets its own animal, and together they form one family.

## The characters

### English Flow: Pip, the London fox
- A London urban fox: clever, curious, a bit cheeky, warm. Dry British humour, never mean.
- Personality in one line: *"the friend who's been in London for years and loves showing you around."*
- Detail that marks it as English Flow: a small **navy scarf** (`hsl(220 62% 34%)`), and optionally a tiny **UK-red** accent (`hsl(354 72% 46%)`), e.g. the scarf's stripe.

### Dutch Flow: Hugo, the Amsterdam heron
- The grey heron that stands by the canals and waits outside fish shops: calm, patient, quietly funny.
- Personality in one line: *"unflappable, a little dramatic about herring, always there when you come back."*
- Detail that marks it as Dutch Flow: a small **orange scarf** (`hsl(24 80% 50%)`).

(Names are suggestions. Propose alternatives if they fit better.)

### Future languages
Same recipe: one well-known animal of the language's main city, same style, same scarf
detail in that app's accent colour. Show one example (e.g. a Madrid magpie for Spanish
Flow) to prove the family scales.

## Style (must match the existing brand)
- Matches the Flow series design: warm cream background `hsl(40 33% 96%)`, dark warm
  brown lines `hsl(20 20% 14%)`, Lora + DM Sans typography around it.
- **Flat, simple, friendly, grown-up.** Think editorial illustration or a modern picture
  book for adults, not a children's cartoon. No huge glossy eyes, no 3D, no gradients.
- Limited palette: the animal's natural colours, toned down to sit with the cream background,
  plus the app's accent on the scarf only.
- Simple geometric shapes so it works small (a 48px avatar) and large (a 240px celebration).
- Consistent line weight with the London skyline illustration already in the app
  (thin, round joins).
- Must work on light and dark backgrounds.

## Personality rules
- Encouraging, never guilt-tripping. It never says or looks "sad because you didn't study".
  Missing a day → calm, welcoming ("Welcome back").
- Celebrates real progress (goal reached, a week of practice, a finished story), not taps.
- Speaks briefly in English with an optional Russian subtitle for English Flow (learners
  are Russian speakers from A1 level).

## Poses (for each character)
1. **Waving / hello:** welcome tour, home greeting.
2. **Happy / proud:** daily goal reached, story finished.
3. **Celebrating:** streak milestones (7, 30, 100 days), level complete. Maybe with small confetti in accent colours.
4. **Thinking / curious:** empty states, "Ask your tutor", loading.
5. **Sleepy / cosy:** "All caught up, come back tomorrow", night-time reminder.
6. **Holding a snowflake:** streak freeze used ("I've kept your streak safe").

## Where it will be used (design one example of each)
- **Welcome tour:** the character guides the 6 tour steps, small next to the text.
- **Home:** a small avatar next to the greeting, with occasional one-line comments
  ("Morning! 10 minutes today?").
- **Celebration sheet:** goal reached / 7-day streak, with a large pose, a short line and one button.
- **Empty state:** "All caught up" on the Cards screen.
- **Notification:** an iOS/Android push mock-up with the character's voice.
- **App icon variant:** the character's head on the app's accent background (an optional alternative to the drop-cap logo, for testing).

**Never in the reading text or exercises.** It mustn't distract from learning.
