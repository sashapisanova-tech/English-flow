# Flow series — Design Brief

Two language-learning apps that should look like one product family:
**Dutch Flow** (teaches Dutch) and **English Flow** (teaches British English to
Russian speakers). Same layout, typography, shapes and mood; each app has its
own accent colour and its own pictures.

## Audience and mood

- Self-learners of all ages, mostly on phones, 10–20 minutes a day.
- English Flow content is young and casual (20s–30s characters, humour, mini-stories).
- Mood: **warm, calm, friendly, grown-up**. Like a cosy café with good books,
  not a children's game. Motivating without being loud.

## Shared by both apps (the "series")

| Element | Current value |
|---|---|
| Heading font | Lora (serif), 500–700 |

| Text | dark warm brown `hsl(20 20% 14%)`, muted `hsl(20 10% 46%)` |

| Success / warning / error | green `hsl(142 60% 40%)` / amber `hsl(38 92% 50%)` / red `hsl(0 72% 51%)` |
| Dark mode | yes, warm dark browns |

## Key screens

1. Login / sign-up (email + "Continue with Google")
2. Home: today's goal, streak, continue reading
3. Reading: text with highlighted key words, tap for translation, questions at the end
4. Flashcards: card front/back, "easy / hard" buttons
5. Profile ("Me"): stats, streak calendar, settings

## Illustration style (one recipe for all images)

To be decided in Claude Design. Requirements: flat or soft-textured, warm
palette matching the cream background, the app's accent colour as the only
strong colour, no photorealism, consistent line weight, people diverse and adult.
