The Flat on Camden Road

Tom has found a flat. It's cheap, it's central, and the landlord is a cat.

Well, not exactly. The landlord is Mrs Hughes, but her cat, Biscuit, makes all the decisions.

'He likes you,' says Mrs Hughes. 'The rent is due on Fridays. Biscuit prefers cash.'

Tom laughs. Then he sees her face. She isn't joking.

The next morning, there is a note under his door. It is signed with a paw.
