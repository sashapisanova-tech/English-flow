Illustration recipe

Flat skyline silhouettes sitting on a single ground line, anchored bottom-right of the card. Fill: accent at 10% over the card colour. Outline: text colour at 38%, 1.4px, round joins. One solid accent object per scene (canal boat, red bus) as the only strong colour. Same frame for both apps: Amsterdam gables for Dutch Flow, Big Ben, Westminster and the London Eye for English Flow.
