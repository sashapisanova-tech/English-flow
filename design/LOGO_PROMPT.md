Design a logo system for **LingoFlow**, a family of language-learning apps.

## The brand family

- **LingoFlow** is the master brand: one company, many languages.
- Each language is its own app with its own logo: **English Flow**, **Dutch Flow**, and later more (e.g. Spanish Flow, German Flow).
- All language logos must clearly belong to LingoFlow: same symbol idea, shape, weight and typography. Only the accent colour and one small language-specific detail change.
- The system must scale: adding a new language should take minutes, not a redesign.

## Who it's for and the feeling

- Adult self-learners of all ages who study 10–20 minutes a day on their phone, without a paid tutor.
- The apps teach through short stories, reading and spaced repetition.
- Feeling: **warm, calm, friendly, grown-up**. Like a cosy café with good books. Not a children's game, not corporate.
- The idea of "flow": steady daily progress, reading that carries you along, language that comes naturally.

## Existing visual identity (keep the logos consistent with it)

- Background: warm cream `hsl(40 33% 96%)`. Text: dark warm brown `hsl(20 20% 14%)`.
- Heading font: **Lora** (serif). Body font: **DM Sans** (sans-serif).
- Rounded shapes, 12px corner radius, soft and calm.
- Accent colours: Dutch Flow is orange `hsl(24 80% 50%)`. English Flow is not decided yet: propose one with the same saturation and lightness and a different hue (blue, green or berry). Future languages follow the same rule.

## What to avoid

- **No flags.** Flags represent countries, not languages (English is spoken in many countries), and they can be politically sensitive.
- No clichés: globes, speech bubbles, graduation caps, owls or other mascots, ABC blocks.
- No fine detail that disappears at small sizes.

## Requirements

- Works as a **phone app icon** (rounded square) and as a **16px browser favicon**.
- Works in one colour (black on white, white on dark) as well as in colour.
- Works on the cream background and in dark mode.
- Simple geometric shapes, suitable for clean SVG.

## Deliverables

1. **Three different concept directions** for the LingoFlow symbol, each with a one-line explanation of the idea.
2. For each direction, show the **whole family side by side**: LingoFlow (master), English Flow, Dutch Flow, plus one imaginary future language (e.g. Spanish Flow) to prove the system scales.
3. For each logo: the symbol alone, the symbol with the wordmark, and the app icon version.
4. A small preview at real sizes: app icon on a phone home screen, 32px and 16px.

Start with the three directions. I'll pick one, and then we'll refine it.
